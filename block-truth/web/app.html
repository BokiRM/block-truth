Closures and disruptions

These are declared. A closed road leaves no trace in delivery records, so somebody has to say it is closed. A closure is driven around - the detour is added and the forecast gets less certain, because nobody has driven that way. Anything else slows the part of a leg that passes through it.

What the estimator learned

Why the earliest and the latest are not the sum of per-stop extremes. Forty stops all landing on their best case is not a fast day, it is a fantasy, and a planner told “between 3h10 and 9h40” has learned nothing. Errors partly cancel: independent noise adds in variance, so a round of N stops has a spread that grows like √N while its length grows like N. But not everything cancels - traffic, weather and who turned up miss the whole day together, and that part does not shrink at all. So the spread is √( Σ per-stop variance + (common mode × duration)² ), and the common-mode fraction is measured from the recorded rounds rather than chosen.
